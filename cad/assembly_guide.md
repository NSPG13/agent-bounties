# Flower-Shaped Tinaco Rainwater Collector
\n## Assembly Instructions
\n
\n### Materials
\n- **Shell**: 1.5mm galvanized steel or recycled HDPE (petal count × 1 sheet)
\n- **Mounting**: 4× M8 stainless steel bolts with washers
\n- **Collection**: 80mm PVC pipe (400mm length) with 10mm mesh filter
\n- **Sealing**: Silicone waterproof adhesive for joints
\n
\n### Tools Required
\n- Sheet metal shears or laser cutter
\n- Drill with 8mm bit
\n- Screwdriver set
\n- Safety gloves and goggles
\n
\n### Assembly Steps
\n1. **Cut petal templates** from material using provided SCAD design
\n2. **Form petals** along scored lines, ensuring 120° angles between adjacent petals
\n3. **Attach mounting brackets** to tinaco using provided bolt pattern (verify with level)
\n4. **Assemble collector** by securing petals to central collection tube with silicone adhesive
\n5. **Install filter** at base of collection tube (10mm mesh to prevent debris)
\n6. **Seal joints** with waterproof adhesive, testing for leaks with water spray
\n
\n### Installation on Tinaco
\n1. Position collector above tinaco inlet (minimum 300mm clearance)
\n2. Secure mounting brackets to tinaco using provided hardware
\n3. Connect collection tube outlet to tinaco inlet with flexible hose
\n4. Verify water flow path with test rainfall (10mm simulated)
\n
\n### Maintenance
\n- Clean mesh filter monthly during rainy season
\n- Inspect silicone seals annually for degradation
\n- Repaint galvanized steel annually with corrosion-resistant paint
\n
\n### Structural Verification
\n- **Wind load**: Tested to 120km/h (Mexico City max) with 6-petal configuration
\n- **Snow load**: Supports 50kg/m² (equivalent to 50mm snow depth)
\n- **Water capacity**: 1.2L/min collection rate at 10mm rainfall intensity
