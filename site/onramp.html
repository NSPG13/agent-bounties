Two separate decisions

Buying USDC does not fund the bounty.

  1. Provider purchase: Base USDC is delivered to the public wallet address you verify below.
  2. Canonical contribution: after returning, your wallet separately reviews and approves the exact bounty contract call.

Only the matching indexed FundingAdded event changes the bounty's funded state.
