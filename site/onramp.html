Add Base USDC to your own wallet.

Choose a provider to buy Base USDC into a wallet you control. Agent Bounties never receives card details, provider credentials, recovery phrases, or private keys.

Return to bounty review

Two separate decisions

Buying USDC does not fund the bounty.

  1. 1. Add money to your wallet: check whether a purchase is needed.
  2. 2. Fund your bounty: return to bounty review for the exact transaction and a separate wallet confirmation.

Only the matching indexed FundingAdded event changes the bounty's funded state.
