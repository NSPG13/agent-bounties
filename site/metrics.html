Definitions and source coverage

Missing or stale required sources are marked partial or delayed instead of being presented as zero.

What is an external active identity?

One namespaced GitHub login, Base wallet, or normalized self-reported comment author with a qualifying action in the period. Namespaces are not merged, so this is participation—not verified unique people.

What counts as payout volume?

Solver reward, verifier reward, and timeout completion bonus from confirmed, block-time-verified BountySettled events across both live protocols, plus verifier pay from confirmed rejected-submission events.

What is a mature claimed round?

An exclusive-claim (network, bounty_id, round) whose deadline has passed or that already has a terminal canonical event. Immature claims are shown separately. Open Competition has no exclusive claim and stays outside this cohort.

What is deliberately excluded?

Bots, maintainers in the public policy, browser visitors, stars, returned claim bonds, refunds, funding intentions, unconfirmed transactions, leaderboard prizes, and private operational analytics.
