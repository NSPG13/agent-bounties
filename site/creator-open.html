    How the money moves
    The creator funds the solver reward plus one review reserve. Each solver posts a bond equal to that reserve. Rejection transfers that submission’s bond to the creator. The first accepted result pays its solver the reward and returns that solver’s bond; the creator receives the unused reserve. Other pending solvers recover their bonds after a winner or their review deadline.
    There is no exclusive claim and no guarantee of payment for work. Closing a bounty and withdrawing its remaining principal are separate sponsored actions. Eligible refunds always go to the original entitled wallet.
    The shared gas allowance resets daily without rollover. Availability can pause when the allowance is used.
