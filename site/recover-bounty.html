Recover bounty funds

Close an unclaimed bounty, then return your contribution to the same wallet. Your wallet asks you to confirm each step.

  1. Cancel the old bounty. This closes it to new claims. Cancellation does not return the funds yet.
  2. Return my funds. The contract sends your contribution and any earned refund bonus back to your wallet.
  3. Review a replacement. Open your saved bounty draft after the refund is confirmed. A replacement is a separate wallet approval.

These calls send 0 ETH to the bounty. Base network fees are additional. No new bounty is funded here.
