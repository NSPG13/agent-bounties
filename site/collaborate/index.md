# Collaborate with Agent Bounties

Welcome! We're excited to partner with you to advance the field of agent-assisted software development. This page outlines how you can contribute to Agent Bounties — whether you're a developer, researcher, or enthusiast — and how we recognize your efforts.

## Why Collaborate?

Agent Bounties is more than a platform; it's a community-driven initiative to bridge the gap between raw AI capabilities and practical software engineering. By collaborating, you help:
- Validate and improve agent-assisted workflows
- Share insights on what works (and what doesn't) in real-world projects
- Contribute to a growing library of reproducible experiments and best practices
- Earn recognition for your contributions — not just financial rewards, but also visibility and impact in the AI+DevOps space

## How to Get Involved

### 1. Participate in the No-Spend Builder Challenge

Our **No-Spend Builder Challenge** invites you to use your own resources (time, compute, data) to experiment with agent-assisted development. The goal is to produce reproducible artifacts — such as successful build logs, optimized prompts, or failure analyses — that others can learn from.

- **Who can join?** Anyone with access to an AI model and a development environment.
- **What do you get?** Public recognition on our Contributors page, potential feature in our newsletter, and the satisfaction of advancing the field.
- **How to submit?** Create a GitHub issue using the "Builder Challenge Submission" template (link below), attach your artifacts, and tag us.

### 2. Contribute to the Collaboration Hub

The **Collaboration Hub** is where we share ongoing projects, open questions, and collaborative opportunities. If you have an idea for a new bounty, a proposed improvement to the platform, or want to share your own agent-assisted project, this is the place.

- **Submit an idea:** Use the "Collaboration Proposal" issue template.
- **Join existing efforts:** Browse open issues labeled “collaboration” and comment with your interest or contributions.
- **Share your work:** If you’ve built something cool using agents, share it here — we’ll help promote it if it aligns with our mission.

### 3. Provide Feedback and Bug Reports

We rely on community feedback to make Agent Bounties better. If you encounter bugs, confusing UI elements, or missing features, please report them via GitHub issues. Your input directly shapes the future of the platform.

## Recognition and Rewards

While the initial phase of Agent Bounties is focused on non-monetary collaboration, we believe in recognizing meaningful contributions:

- **Public acknowledgment:** Contributors will be listed on our website’s Contributors page with links to their profiles and work.
- **Featured stories:** Exceptional submissions may be highlighted in our blog or newsletter.
- **Future incentives:** As the platform grows, we plan to introduce tiered rewards (e.g., badges, priority access, or even tokenized credits) for consistent and impactful contributors.

## Getting Started

Ready to dive in? Here’s what to do next:

1. **Read the guidelines:** Review the [Builder Challenge Rules](https://github.com/NSPG13/agent-bounties/blob/main/docs/builder-challenge.md) and [Collaboration Hub Guidelines](https://github.com/NSPG13/agent-bounties/blob/main/docs/collaboration-hub.md).
2. **Choose your path:** Decide whether to submit a builder challenge, propose a collaboration, or report an issue.
3. **Create an issue:** Use the appropriate template from the links below.
4. **Wait for review:** We’ll acknowledge your submission within 48 hours and provide next steps.
