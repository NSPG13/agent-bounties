How pooled refunds work

Each funder calls withdrawRefund from the wallet that contributed. The contract returns that wallet's principal plus its pro-rata share of any forfeited timeout-bond pool. The creator cannot withdraw another funder's money.

BoundedAgentWalletV2 lets its owner atomically cancel an unclaimed bounty and recover that wallet's share. It does not grant platform maintainers control over another user's wallet or pooled contributions.

What cannot be reversed

After a claim, cancellation is blocked. After BountySettled, the solver and verifier transfers are final under the contract; maintainers have no reversal or chargeback function.

Evidence

Only confirmed canonical BountyCancelled and RefundWithdrawn events prove cancellation and refund. A plan, wallet prompt, broadcast, or transaction hash alone does not.
